Radiant Lighting started as a small lighting supplier in Multan. Over the years we've lit family homes, offices, shops and restaurants, and learned what works in Pakistani homes and climates.

Today we carry our own range of downlights, laser blades, track lights, strip lights and profiles, alongside trusted brands. Every product is one we'd put in our own home.
